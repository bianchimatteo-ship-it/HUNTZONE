# HUNTZONE — Product vision

## Purpose

HUNTZONE makes familiar pursuit and hiding games work across real places and connected devices. A match creates a bounded play area, assigns roles, applies one shared set of rules, and lets players act on incomplete information. The map anchors the experience; suspense, teamwork and bluffing make the platform broader than hide-and-seek.

## Product principles

1. **The real world stays in charge.** The game never rewards unsafe speed, trespass or attention to a screen while moving. Every elimination and hazard is virtual.
2. **Start quickly.** A player can join a private match with a code or link and a guest identity. The host can start with a small number of clear choices.
3. **Rules are legible.** Roles, capture conditions, location visibility and match state are explained before play. The server resolves disputes consistently.
4. **Location is purpose-limited.** Sharing starts only after an informed match opt-in and stops at match end. Exact coordinates are hidden from other players unless a mode explicitly needs them.
5. **Modes compose from trusted mechanics.** New modes combine versioned rules and validated settings; they do not run arbitrary scripts.
6. **Clients can evolve independently.** Web, Android and future iOS clients speak the same versioned API and do not own game outcomes.

## Audience and jobs to be done

- **Friends and families:** arrange a spontaneous game in a park or other suitable public place.
- **Organizers and clubs:** host repeatable sessions, tune boundaries and keep a group together.
- **Competitive players:** learn official modes, improve through fair statistics and join public matches.
- **Mode creators:** modify a known mode or publish a safe combination of supported rules.

The initial audience is adults playing with people they know. Public matchmaking, minors, broad social discovery and persistent community features need additional moderation, privacy and safeguarding decisions before launch.

## Positioning and identity

HUNTZONE is a GPS Hide & Seek Games platform: international, mysterious, competitive and immersive. Visual direction: high-contrast charcoal/black surfaces, restrained signal red, tactical green and alert yellow, with a neutral light palette where accessibility requires it. Use military-inspired information hierarchy without weapons imagery or instructions that imply real conflict. Keep maps readable and controls large enough to use outdoors.

Possible slogan for testing: **Hide. Hunt. Outlast.** It is concise and portable across modes. Alternative to test in research: **The city is your game.** Neither slogan nor logo is approved. Commission or create a distinctive HUNTZONE mark and test it at small mobile sizes before adoption. Check name, domain, store and trademark availability before commercial release.

## Experience and business boundaries

The product begins as a web experience, then adds a native Android location client, while keeping accounts, match state and game rules on one backend. iOS should reuse that backend and protocol. Revenue may later come from non-invasive advertising, cosmetic customization and premium host tools. Purchases must not change competitive outcomes; do not introduce pay-to-win powers, paid location advantages or paid revives.

## Success measures to validate

- Time from opening the site to a ready lobby and from lobby to match start.
- Successful joins and match completion, segmented by client and connectivity.
- Reconnect rate and percentage of games that recover without host intervention.
- Location permission comprehension, denial and withdrawal rates.
- Safety reports per match and rate of resolved reports.
- Repeat sessions and creator reuse of saved modes.

Set numerical targets only after prototype tests establish a baseline. Do not optimize for raw location-update volume or time spent looking at the map.
